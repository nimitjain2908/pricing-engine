CONTRACT TERMS AND BILLING POLICY
FinSight Legal and Finance Team — 2026

BILLING CYCLES

Monthly Billing:
- Charged on the same date each month (anniversary billing)
- No long-term commitment
- Full price — no discount
- Can upgrade or downgrade at any time
- Cancellation effective at end of current billing period
- No refunds for partial months

Annual Billing:
- 20% discount applied to all tiers
- Paid upfront for full 12 months
- Can upgrade mid-year (prorated charge for remainder of term)
- Cannot downgrade mid-year
- Cancellation: No refund for remaining months (store credit offered for extenuating circumstances)
- Auto-renews unless cancelled 30 days before renewal date

Multi-Year Billing (Enterprise only):
- 2-year: 25% discount
- 3-year: 30% discount
- Custom payment schedules available (annual instalments)
- Price locked for full contract term — no price increases
- Early termination fee: 50% of remaining contract value

FREE TRIALS
- 14-day free trial on Starter and Growth
- No credit card required for trial
- Full feature access during trial
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate target: 22%
- Trial extension policy: Up to 7 additional days with Sales approval

ENTERPRISE PILOT TERMS
- Paid pilots only (minimum $500 for 30-day pilot)
- Pilot fee credited against first year contract if signed
- Pilot must include defined success criteria agreed upfront
- No free pilots without VP Sales approval

REFUND POLICY
- Monthly plans: No refunds
- Annual plans: Refund within 14 days of annual renewal if requested (minus usage)
- Enterprise: Per contract terms
- Exceptions: Technical failures causing significant downtime (beyond SLA breach thresholds) eligible for prorated credit

PRICE INCREASE POLICY
- 60 days written notice required for any price increase
- Annual contract customers: Price increase effective at next renewal only
- Monthly customers: Price increase effective 60 days after notice
- Maximum increase without individual negotiation: 15% per year
- Increases above 15% require customer success outreach before notice
