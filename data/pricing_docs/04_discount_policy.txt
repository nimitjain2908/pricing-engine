DISCOUNT AND PRICING POLICY
FinSight Internal Document — Confidential
Effective: January 2026

STANDARD DISCOUNTS (No approval required)

Annual Plan Discount:
- 20% discount for annual upfront payment
- Applied automatically at checkout
- Non-negotiable, non-stackable with other discounts

Nonprofit Discount:
- 30% discount for registered nonprofits
- Requires verification of nonprofit status
- Applied to any tier, annual or monthly

Startup Discount:
- 25% discount for companies under 2 years old with under $1M ARR
- Requires proof of incorporation date
- Valid for first 12 months only, then full price applies

NEGOTIATED DISCOUNTS (Sales approval required)

Volume Discount — Growth Tier:
- 5% additional discount for 3-year commitment
- 10% additional discount for 5+ seat purchases on Enterprise
- Requires VP Sales approval

Competitive Displacement Discount:
- Up to 20% additional discount when displacing a named competitor
- Must provide proof of competitor contract or invoice
- Valid for first contract term only
- Requires Sales Director approval

DISCOUNTS THAT REQUIRE CEO APPROVAL
- Any discount exceeding 40% of list price
- Free or heavily subsidised pilots exceeding 6 months
- Custom pricing below Enterprise floor of $499/month

DISCOUNT STACKING RULES
- Maximum two discounts can be stacked
- Annual discount can be stacked with one other discount
- Total discount cannot exceed 45% of list price without CEO approval

DISCOUNT ABUSE PREVENTION
- All discounts must be logged in CRM with justification
- Discounts above 25% require documented competitive intelligence
- Quarterly audit of discount patterns by Finance team
- Sales commission calculated on discounted ARR to prevent over-discounting

PRICING EXCEPTIONS PROCESS
1. Sales rep submits pricing exception request in CRM
2. Sales Manager reviews within 24 hours
3. VP Sales approves discounts up to 35%
4. CEO approves discounts above 35%
5. All exceptions logged for quarterly review
