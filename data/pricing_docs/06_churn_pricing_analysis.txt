CHURN AND PRICING CORRELATION ANALYSIS
FinSight Data Science Team — Q1 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Analysis of 3,200 customer accounts over 24 months reveals strong correlations between pricing decisions, plan type, and churn behaviour. Key finding: contract length is the single strongest predictor of retention, followed by feature adoption depth.

CHURN RATES BY PLAN

Starter Plan:
- Monthly churn rate: 8.2%
- Annual churn rate: 4.1%
- Primary churn reason: Price (42%), Found free alternative (28%), Business closed (18%), Missing features (12%)
- Average customer lifetime: 14 months (monthly billing), 22 months (annual billing)

Growth Plan:
- Monthly churn rate: 3.8%
- Annual churn rate: 1.9%
- Primary churn reason: Missing features (35%), Price increase (25%), Competitor switch (22%), Business downturn (18%)
- Average customer lifetime: 28 months (monthly billing), 44 months (annual billing)

Enterprise Plan:
- Annual churn rate: 6.5% (higher due to deliberate offboarding of low-fit accounts)
- Primary churn reason: Consolidation to ERP (45%), Budget cuts (30%), Competitor (25%)
- Average customer lifetime: 38 months

IMPACT OF PRICE INCREASES ON CHURN

Historical price increase analysis:
- 2023: 8% price increase on Starter — churn increased 1.2% in following quarter, normalised after 2 quarters
- 2024: 12% price increase on Growth — churn increased 2.8%, did not fully normalise (net negative)
- 2025: 5% price increase with feature additions — churn decreased 0.3% (customers perceived value increase)

Key insight: Price increases without accompanying feature additions or value communication drive lasting churn increases. Price increases paired with genuine value additions can be neutral or positive for retention.

FEATURE ADOPTION AND CHURN CORRELATION
Customers using 3+ integrations: 1.8x less likely to churn
Customers using forecasting feature: 2.1x less likely to churn
Customers using API: 3.2x less likely to churn
Customers who attended onboarding webinar: 1.6x less likely to churn

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Prioritise driving feature adoption in first 90 days — especially forecasting and integrations
2. Any price increase above 8% must be paired with new features or risk lasting churn increase
3. Annual billing incentives should be increased to drive monthly-to-annual conversion
4. At-risk customers (low feature adoption + monthly billing) should receive proactive outreach before renewal
