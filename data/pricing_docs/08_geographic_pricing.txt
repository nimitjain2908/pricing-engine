GEOGRAPHIC PRICING STRATEGY
FinSight Global Expansion Team — Q1 2026

OVERVIEW
As FinSight expands beyond the US market, pricing must reflect local purchasing power, competitive dynamics, and willingness to pay by geography. This document outlines our geographic pricing framework.

PRICING BY REGION

North America (Base pricing):
- Starter: $45/month
- Growth: $139/month
- Enterprise: Starting $499/month
- Market maturity: High. Customers expect full feature sets and strong support.

United Kingdom:
- Starter: £38/month (approximately 15% below US PPP-adjusted)
- Growth: £115/month
- Enterprise: Starting £399/month
- Notes: Strong accounting software market. Xero dominates. Must emphasise integrations with UK-specific tools (FreeAgent, Sage).

European Union:
- Starter: €42/month
- Growth: €125/month
- Enterprise: Starting €449/month
- Notes: GDPR compliance is a strong selling point. Data residency in EU is a requirement for many customers. Price in local currency where possible.

India:
- Starter: ₹2,499/month (approximately 70% discount to US pricing on PPP basis)
- Growth: ₹7,999/month
- Enterprise: Starting ₹29,999/month
- Notes: Fast-growing market. Price sensitivity is high. GST compliance features are a must-have. Partnership with local accounting firms recommended.

Southeast Asia:
- Pricing at 60-65% of US pricing across tiers
- Singapore is exception — price at 85% of US pricing (higher WTP)
- Notes: Localisation of currency and tax compliance features required before launch.

PURCHASING POWER PARITY ADJUSTMENT FRAMEWORK
We use a PPP adjustment factor for each market:
- US: 1.0 (base)
- UK: 0.85
- EU: 0.88
- Canada: 0.90
- Australia: 0.92
- India: 0.28
- Southeast Asia: 0.55-0.70

CURRENCY AND BILLING
- All non-US billing in local currency where Stripe supports it
- Exchange rate locked at contract signing for annual deals
- Monthly deals subject to exchange rate fluctuation with 30-day notice of significant changes
