EXPANSION REVENUE AND UPSELL PRICING STRATEGY
FinSight Revenue Team — Q2 2026

OVERVIEW
Expansion revenue (revenue from existing customers through upgrades, add-ons, and seat expansions) currently represents 28% of new ARR. Industry benchmark for best-in-class SaaS is 35-40%. This document outlines pricing strategy to close that gap.

CURRENT EXPANSION REVENUE BREAKDOWN
- Plan upgrades (Starter to Growth): 45% of expansion ARR
- Plan upgrades (Growth to Enterprise): 30% of expansion ARR
- Seat expansions within tier: 15% of expansion ARR
- Add-on purchases: 10% of expansion ARR

UPGRADE TRIGGERS AND PRICING

Starter to Growth upgrade triggers:
- User limit reached (most common — 38% of upgrades)
- Transaction limit reached (22% of upgrades)
- Request for API access (18% of upgrades)
- Request for forecasting (15% of upgrades)
- Other (7%)

Recommendation: Surface upgrade prompts at 80% of limit utilisation, not 100%. Earlier prompting increases conversion.

Growth to Enterprise upgrade triggers:
- Multi-entity requirement (most common)
- SSO requirement from IT team
- Dedicated support request
- White labelling request

ADD-ON PRICING STRATEGY

Current add-ons:
- Additional API calls: $29/month per 10,000 additional calls
- Advanced audit log: $19/month
- Custom data retention (7 years): $29/month

Proposed new add-ons:
- AI Insights Pro: $49/month — AI-generated monthly insights report
- Priority onboarding: $299 one-time — dedicated onboarding specialist for 4 sessions
- Custom integrations: $199/month — one custom integration built and maintained

Add-on pricing philosophy: Add-ons should be priced at 20-30% of the base plan price to feel like a reasonable incremental spend, not a significant new commitment.

NET REVENUE RETENTION TARGET
Current NRR: 108%
Target NRR: 118% by end of 2026
Strategy: Drive 40% more Growth-to-Enterprise upgrades through proactive CSM outreach at 12-month mark, and launch AI Insights Pro add-on in Q3 2026.
